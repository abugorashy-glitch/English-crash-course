Correct Answer: B       I must re-press the wrinkled suit.
Explanation: use a hyphen with the prefix re when re means
 “again,” and omitting the hyphen would cause confusion with a 
similar word with a different meaning.


الإجابة الصحيحة: I must re-press the wrinkled suit.
