Correct Answer: A       Ahmad is a two-games-a-day chess fanatic.
Explanation: Hyphenate two or more words when they come before a noun they modify and act as a single idea. The noun in this case is “fanatic”; “chess” is an adjective but is not part of “two-games-a-day,” the compound adjective.
