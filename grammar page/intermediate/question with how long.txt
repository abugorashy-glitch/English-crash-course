How long + has + (He, She, It, Ram, Sita) + been + verb + ing? 

1. How long has he been waiting? 
2. How long has he been playing? 
3. How long has Ram been reading? 
4. How long has she been singing? 
5. How long has she been writing? 
6. How long has it been working? 
7. How long has it been producing? 
8. How long has he been standing? 


Corresponding Answers : 

He has been waiting for 3 hours. 
He has been playing for 2 hours
Ram has been reading for 3 hours
She has been singing for 2 hours
She has been writing for 3 hours. 
It has been working for 10 hours. 
It has been producing five tonnes. 
He has been standing for 20 minutes.
