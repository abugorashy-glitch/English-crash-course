When giving instructions, it's important to use the correct grammar. 
Here are some key points to remember:

Use the imperative mood: This is the command form of a verb. 
For example, instead of saying "You should turn left," say "Turn left."
Use clear and concise language: Avoid using complex vocabulary or long sentences.
Keep your instructions simple and easy to understand.
Use active voice: Active voice is more direct and easier to follow than passive voice. 
For example, instead of saying "The door should be closed," say "Close the door."
