Phrasal Verb 'Stem'
We have 1 phrasal verb definition related to 'Stem'.
Stem from
Meaning: Originate, be caused by
المعني: ينشأ‘ يسبب بواسطة
Example: The trouble STEMS FROM their refusal to discuss the matter.
مثال: تنشأ المشكلة من رفضهم مناقشة المسألة
Notes:
