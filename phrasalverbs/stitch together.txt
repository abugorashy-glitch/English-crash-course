Phrasal Verb 'Stitch'
Stitch together
Meaning: Make a deal, create something quickly and without attention to detail
المعني: اعمل صفقة‘ انشيء شيئا ما بسرعة ودون الانتباه للتفاصيل
Example: They STITCHED TOGETHER an agreement to form a coalition.
مثال: عملوا اتفاقية ليكونوا اتحاد
Notes:
⦁	Separable [optional] 
⦁	British English
